Garden Collection

Inspired by the beauty and fragrance of the garden, our Garden Collection brings together handcrafted soaps designed to evoke timeless floral charm.

Antique Rose

Simple • Elegant • Timeless

Step into a traditional English garden with a soap that combines the old-world luxury of rich goat milk with the benefits of natural rose clay. With a dense, creamy lather, it deeply hydrates while gently cleansing. Its soft pink color, classic rose stamp, and graceful floral fragrance were designed to evoke the timeless elegance of an English garden.

Ingredients: Olive Oil, Coconut Oil, Lard, Water, Goat Milk, Sodium Hydroxide, Essential Oils for Fragrance (Rose Geranium, Lavender, Patchouli, Cedarwood, Orange), Rose Clay, Rosemary oleoresin antioxidant.

Sassy Grasshopper

Fresh • Herbal • Refreshing

Fresh as a summer garden after the rain, Sassy Grasshopper combines crisp peppermint with aromatic rosemary for a scent that awakens the senses. Made with farm-fresh goat milk, mineral-rich kaolin clay, and a touch of sea salt, this creamy bar leaves skin feeling soft, clean, and refreshed.

Ingredients: Olive Oil, Coconut Oil, Lard, Water, Sodium Hydroxide, Goat Milk, Essential Oils (Peppermint, Rosemary), Sea Salt, Grasshopper Mica, Kaolin Clay, Rosemary oleoresin antioxidant.

Lavender Haze

Soft • Serene • Dreamy

Inspired by the quiet beauty of the French countryside, Lavender Haze is a soothing bar with a soft, romantic character. Goat milk and kaolin clay complement our traditional base oils to create a creamy, luxurious lather, while indigo-infused olive oil and a touch of mineral color give each bar its softly muted, sage-toned hue. Three delicate lavender stems, hand-stamped and lightly accented in deep lavender, complete its botanical character.

The fragrance combines classic French lavender, softened by floral rose geranium and grounded with warm cedarwood. The result is more nuanced than lavender alone—calming and floral, yet earthy enough to feel beautifully balanced.

Ingredients: Olive Oil, Coconut Oil, Lard, Goat Milk, Sodium Hydroxide, Water, Indigo Infused Olive Oil, Essential Oils for Fragrance (Lavender, Rose Geranium, Cedarwood), Kaolin Clay, Ultramarines Blue, Sea Salt.

$10.00

Misty Morning

Quiet • Earthy • Serene

Based on the hushed beauty of an early morning wrapped in mist, Misty Morning pairs a natural creamy base with a softly mottled lavender-gray sky. Indigo and madder root create the muted color, while a gentle swirl allows the two layers to drift into one another like morning fog settling over the landscape.

Goat milk and kaolin clay complement our signature base oils to create a creamy, luxurious lather with a smooth, silky feel. The fragrance begins with classic French lavender, softened by floral rose geranium and grounded with warm, woody cedarwood. Calming and floral with an earthy undertone, the blend perfectly suits the quiet, misty character of an Acadian morning.

Ingredients: Olive Oil, Coconut Oil, Lard, Goat Milk, Sodium Hydroxide, Water, Essential Oils (Lavender, Geranium, Cedarwood), Kaolin Clay, Sea Salt, Indigo Powder, Madder Root Powder.
